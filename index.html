01 — About Us

Passion for engineering.
Obsession with craft.

Iron & Oil is a small, hard-working performance shop out of Detroit. We live for cold starts at midnight, the smell of cutting oil, and a perfectly torqued head stud.

We build fast things right. That means clean fabrication, honest diagnostics, and tuning that holds up lap after lap. No corporate fluff. Just mechanics who care.
